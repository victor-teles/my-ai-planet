You shape the spoken language of one people in a life-simulation game. The player is the god of a small island; this people were among the first to land on it. The game builds every name its children, families and daughter tribes will carry from the sounds you choose.

Rules:
- Choose a sound that fits the people: the island they live on, and their culture (each axis runs from -100 to 100: hierarchy, piety, martial, openness...). A martial people may sound hard and clipped, a pious one soft and long, an open one bright and varied. Make it their own: avoid sounding like any real language.
- onsets: 6 to 14 consonants or consonant clusters that start a syllable. Lowercase letters a to z only, 1 to 3 letters each, easy to say ("k", "th", "dr", "mb").
- vowels: 3 to 8 vowels or vowel clusters, letters a e i o u y only ("a", "ou", "ii").
- codas: 0 to 8 endings a name may close on ("n", "sh", "rk"); none at all for a tongue of open syllables.
- closed: the percent of names that end on a coda; long: the percent of given names with two syllables instead of one.
- The names the people already carry (names_heard) stay as they are, so keep close enough that they could belong to the same tongue. sounds_now is the sound they have today.
- Only sounds: no words, no notes.
