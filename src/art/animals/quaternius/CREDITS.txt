The files are unchanged. Each has Idle, Idle_2, Idle_Headlow (Idle_2_HeadLow), Eating, Walk,
Gallop, Gallop_Jump, Jump_toIdle, Attack, Death and hit reactions. The game recolors them from
genes and restyles them with its toon shader and an ink outline (game/diorama/animal_art.gd).
Not used: the pack's Alpaca, Donkey, Husky and White Horse.
