Two rigged, animated animals from OpenGameArt.org, both CC0 1.0 (public domain).

boar.glb: "Boar" by Teh_Bucket (2015). Rigged, with walk and attack animations.
  https://opengameart.org/content/boar  (boar_0.blend)
goat.glb: "3D goat animation for game" by tomek (2022).
  https://opengameart.org/content/3d-goat-animation-for-game  (goat_0.blend)
  Clips: idle, walk, run, jump, fly, land, swim, eat, attack, die, turn left and right.

Converted from .blend to .glb with Blender 5.2 (scripts disabled): only the armature and its
mesh kept, modifiers applied, every action exported. The painted textures were dropped: each
face took a flat named material (Main, Horns, Muzzle, Hair, Eye_Black) from the texture color
at its UV center, and the horn and tusk faces became their own mesh pinned to the head bone,
so the game can recolor the coat from genes and grow the horns with the horns gene.
